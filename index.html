Giovanni Varricchione

Hi! I am a postdoc at Utrecht University, working with Mehdi Dastani and Roxana Rădulescu. My postdoc is embedded in the DECIDE project, and I will work on explainable (multi-objective) reinforcement learning.

I am also volunteering at SAIN Utrecht, where I help the local chapter's education team in promoting techniques and ideas to make AI safer.

Previously, I was a PhD candidate at Utrecht University, under the supervision of Natasha Alechina, Mehdi Dastani, and Brian Logan. I worked at the intersection between reinforcement learning, logic, and formal methods, with the goal of improving sample efficiency and safety of learning.
